Method: content/research/METHOD.md, including "Lessons from Domain 3" and "Citations for the source directory".
Every factual claim in the existing briefing (src/lib/articles.ts, id 1.6) was listed and checked against the
primary source text. Every web source below was fetched with curl and read as raw page text with the tags

quotation, the figures and all ten reading-list quotes were matched as exact strings using the project's own

## Not verified, and cautions

- The EU dates rest on the European Commission's own copies, not the Official Journal, which could not be read. The
  Service Desk article pages say the text follows the EUR-Lex consolidated version as at 27 July 2026, yet their
  footer still reads "Official version of 13 June 2024". Both notices are recorded; the amended text was used.
- "The speech of May 2026, as published, does not mention artificial intelligence" is a statement about the speech
  text on gov.uk only. Background briefing notes to the speech were not read. Parliament's bills site was blocked,
  so I cannot say whether any AI bill is before Parliament.
- "kept to existing law applied by existing regulators" summarises a 2024 document of the previous government. No
  later full statement of the UK framework was found in this pass.
- "Thirteen bodies" is my own count of a list, so it is not offered as the brief's figure.
- The figure is taken from the as-made version of the instrument. Earlier and later commencement regulations for
  the same Act exist (this one is No. 6) and were not read.
- The ICO page prints no date; the "now in force" statement was on the page on 2 October 2026.
- Body word count: 286. Reading list: 10 items.
