# 8.1 Value-chain position (researcher log, 2 October 2026)

Method: content/research/METHOD.md, including "Lessons from Domain 3" and "Citations for the source directory".
Every claim in the existing briefing (src/lib/articles.ts, same id) was listed and checked against source text read
on 2 October 2026. Web pages were fetched with curl and read as raw text with the tags stripped; PDFs were read as
text extracted from the PDF itself. No summarising tool was used. The quotation, the figures and every reading-list
quote were then re-fetched and matched as exact strings (after normalising white space and quote marks).

## Not verified, and cautions

- "No UK law was found that limits what a customer may ask" is a negative finding from the sources read, not a full
  search of UK law.
- The 85% is the Commission services' estimate. The basis of the estimate was not traced.
- The brief says central government "asks bidders"; PPN 006 says in-scope bodies "should take action to apply" it,
  where relevant and proportionate. Devolved and local bodies were not checked.
- The Commission page and the gov.uk UK SRS page are both behind events; each reading-list entry says so.
- Who reports when, for an individual EU customer, also depends on national transposition and on the optional
  exemption for financial years 2025 and 2026. Not researched country by country.
- Name of the UK department: gov.uk pages print "Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade" in the
  header of pages first published by the Department for Business and Trade. The reading list uses the name printed
  on the document itself and notes the header.
- Body word count: 286 including the six bold labels (267 without). Reading list: 10 items.
