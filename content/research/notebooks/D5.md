## The senior responsible individual question

Established from the statute, not from commentary. The enacted Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 contains no
"senior responsible individual": the phrase does not appear anywhere in the Act's table of contents, and UK GDPR
Article 37 ("Designation of the data protection officer") is still in force. Its only 2025 Act amendment is the
insertion of "and tribunals" in 37(1)(a) (20.8.2025, Sch. 11 para. 11). The data protection officer therefore
remains the statutory role. The brief does not mention either role.
