- "No UK law was found that limits what a customer may ask" is a negative finding from the sources read, not a full
  search of UK law.
- The 85% is the Commission services' estimate. The basis of the estimate was not traced.
- The brief says central government "asks bidders"; PPN 006 says in-scope bodies "should take action to apply" it,
  where relevant and proportionate. Devolved and local bodies were not checked.
- The Commission page and the gov.uk UK SRS page are both behind events; each reading-list entry says so.

  header of pages first published by the Department for Business and Trade. The reading list uses the name printed

## Not verified, and cautions

  observation about investment-grade borrowers in a market of large loans, three years old, and the letter itself
  says the regulator does not directly regulate that market. It is used to show how small the price effect has been
  where it has been observed, not as a rate a small firm can expect. No later FCA publication on the subject was
  searched for.

  brief's "must supply" is the bidder's position where the condition is applied. The lead may prefer "is normally
  required to supply".
- PPN 002 and PPN 006 bind central government bodies only. The brief says "central government" each time.

  pass or fail rule. It is not a measured finding.
