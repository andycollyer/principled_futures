# 1.2 Roles and decision rights (researcher log, 2 October 2026)

Method: content/research/METHOD.md, including "Lessons from Domain 3" and "Citations for the source directory".
Every web source was fetched with curl and read as raw page text with the tags stripped; PDFs were read as text
extracted from the PDF itself. A search tool was used only to find two URLs (the FRC annual review and the DSIT
adoption research); both documents were then read at source. The quotation, the figure and every reading-list quote
were matched as exact strings in the raw text after normalising white space and quote marks.

Angle. The named owner and the escalation route. The board's own remit is left to the mandate brief. The legacy
briefing spends most of its length on the test for meaningful human involvement, which the Domain 3 and Domain 5
briefs already cover; this brief keeps to who is answerable, and puts the automated-decision material in the reading
list only.

Neither string is in content/sources/anchors.json. The white paper URL is already the source of the redress brief's
quotation, which is a different sentence. The brief shortens "named accountable responsible owner" to "named
accountable owner" outside quotation marks.

Paraphrased passages the brief rests on:
- "in larger firms any use of AI falls within an existing senior manager's responsibilities": FCA AI Update para 3.40,
  "any use of AI in relation to an activity, business area, or management function of a firm would fall within the
  scope of a SMF manager’s responsibilities". This is said of PRA-authorised firms and FCA Enhanced firms, "but not
  Core or Limited Scope SM&CR firms"; hence "in larger firms".
- "most respondents ... opposed a separate responsibility for AI": FS2/23 para 4.17, "Most respondents did not think
  that creating a new Prescribed Responsibility (PR) for AI to be allocated to a Senior Management Function (SMF)
  would be helpful". DP5/22 was published in October 2022.
- PRA: SS1/23 Principle 2.2, "Firms should identify a relevant SMF(s) ... to assume overall responsibility for the MRM
  framework".
- Government guidance: AI Playbook, "Nominate a senior responsible owner (SRO) who will be accountable for the use of
  AI in a specific project."

## Not verified, and cautions

- The white paper was published under the previous government. The principle is quoted as the stated cross-sector
  position; whether the present government has restated it was not checked beyond the February 2024 response.
- The FCA AI Update is from April 2024. A later FCA statement on AI and the SM&CR may exist; not searched.
- The NAO figure is self-reported, from autumn 2023, and covers government bodies only. No comparable figure for
  companies was found. It shares a paragraph with the "15 of 32" finding, which this brief does not use.
- "independently reviewed" at the top level of the criterion has no source specific to AI roles.
- The OECD page prints no date; the reading list gives 2019 as the year of adoption, not confirmed on that page.
- Body word count 281 including the six bold labels. Reading list: 10 items.
