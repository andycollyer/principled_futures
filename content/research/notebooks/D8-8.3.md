# 8.3 Comprehensive readiness (researcher log, 2 October 2026)

Method: content/research/METHOD.md, including "Lessons from Domain 3" and "Citations for the source directory".
Every claim in the existing briefing (src/lib/articles.ts, same id) was listed and checked against source text read
on 2 October 2026. Web pages were fetched with curl and read as raw text with the tags stripped; PDFs were read as
text extracted from the PDF itself. No summarising tool was used. The quotation, the figures and every reading-list
quote were then re-fetched and matched as exact strings (after normalising white space and quote marks).

## Not verified, and cautions

- The brief says the FCA put Scope 3 on a comply-or-explain basis "recognising that many struggle to obtain the
  data". Paragraph 2.10 uses those words of the consultation proposal. The final rules apply comply or explain to
  the whole framework, with one year's relief for Scope 3. The sentence is accurate but compresses this.
- "Those companies will turn to suppliers" is inference from the Scope 3 categories, not a quoted finding.
- "The emissions of bought-in AI services sit in the value chain" is reasoning from the Scope 3 definition
  (purchased goods and services), not a sourced statement about AI.
- The criterion's level text lists "strategy, value-chain emissions, targets, climate risks, human rights". Value-chain
  emissions are kept in the brief because the criterion names them, with the correction that the standard treats
  them as optional.
- Cost figures: Commission estimate for EU firms, from a cost-benefit analysis of the 2024 standard; not UK costs,
  and not valid above 250 employees.
- Body word count: 283 including the six bold labels (264 without). Reading list: 9 items.
