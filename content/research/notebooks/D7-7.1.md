### EU AI Act: what was read and how dates were established
- EUR-Lex (HTML and PDF addresses for OJ:L_202401689) returned HTTP 202 with an empty body. Not read. The
  Digital Omnibus amending regulation was not found in the Official Journal in this pass; its number is not known.
- European Commission AI Act Service Desk pages were read instead (Articles 4, 14, 26, 113 and the timeline).
  Each article page carries the banner "This provision has been amended by the Digital Omnibus on AI. The text
  displayed on this page has not yet been updated to reflect those amendments." Yet Articles 4 and 113 display
  paragraphs labelled "AMENDED" (and "NEW") with wording that matches the Commission's own description of the
  amendment. Articles 14 and 26 show no "AMENDED" label on any paragraph. The banner and the labels contradict
  each other; the labelled text is used because it is the more precise, and the contradiction is stated in the
  reading list.
- Dates, all from the Service Desk: Article 113 (a) "Chapters I and II shall apply from 2 February 2025";
  (c)(i) "2 December 2027 as regards AI systems classified as high-risk pursuant to Article 6(2) and Annex III";
  (c)(ii) "2 August 2028" for Annex I. Timeline page: "02 Feb 2025 General provisions (definitions & AI
  literacy) and prohibitions apply"; "02 Aug 2026 ... Enforcement of the AI Act starts at national and EU-level
  concerning general-purpose AI models, prohibitions, transparency rules and AI literacy"; "02 Dec 2027 Rules for
  high-risk AI systems in Annex III apply".
- Commission AI literacy Q&A (digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu): says the Omnibus amendments to Article 4 "entered
  into force in mid-July 2026"; a Commission news item listed on the Omnibus proposal page says "On 27 July 2026,
  the AI Omnibus enters into force". The Q&A gives the enforcement start as "3 August 2026" in one answer and
  "2 August 2026" in the next; the briefs say "August 2026". Its opening line still describes the old wording
  ("ensure a sufficient level of AI literacy").
