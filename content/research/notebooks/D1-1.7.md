## Not verified, and cautions

- "No general law requires an independent audit of AI governance" is a negative. It rests on the sources read (the
  Code, data protection law, government assurance policy), not on a search of all sector rules. Financial services
  model risk rules, medical device rules and the EU AI Act's conformity assessment were not researched here.
- The quotation reports qualitative research with members of the public; the report gives no sample size at that
  point.
- The figure is an estimate from November 2024 and counts suppliers, not buyers. The 2025 roadmap restates the
  market as worth over £1 billion with about 12,500 employees and does not update the firm count.
- The BSI claim to be first is the company's own. Whether other bodies are now accredited was not checked.
- The IIA standards file is marked for individual personal use; it is the free copy on the IIA's own site.
- The IIA AI framework prints no date; the year rests on its copyright line (2024) and file name.
- ICO guidance cited is under review.
- Body word count: 285. Reading list: 10 items.
