# Domain 2, criterion 2.6 Incident readiness and suspension: research log

(researcher log, 2 October 2026; not yet re-checked by the lead)

Method: content/research/METHOD.md in full, including "Lessons from Domain 3", "Citations for the source
directory" and the 2 October 2026 update on the Commission's consolidated copy of the EU AI Act. Every factual
claim in the legacy briefing (src/lib/articles.ts, id 2.6) was listed and checked against source text. Pages and
PDFs were downloaded with curl; HTML tags were stripped and PDF text was extracted from the file itself with
pypdf. The quotation, the figure and all ten reading-list quotes were then confirmed by exact-string match using
the project's own fetch and normalisation (scripts/source_check.py: fetch, norm), so they are tested the way the
fortnightly check will test them. Web search was used only to find addresses; nothing is cited from a search
summary. content/sources/anchors.json was read first; nothing listed there is reused, and no quotation or figure
is shared between the four briefs 2.5 to 2.8. EU AI Act: read only from the Commission's AI Act Service Desk
copy, each article page stating "based on the EUR-Lex consolidated version of the AI Act as at 27 July 2026".

Angle: risk governance (who may suspend, when the duty to report arises, to whom). Security incident response,
kill-switch engineering and resilience are left to Domain 6. For that reason the brief does not print the
72-hour limit in UK GDPR Art. 33, which Domain 6 is likely to use; it is recorded in the reading list only.

| 2 | Ten days where death is involved | supported | Art. 73(4); also "as soon as it suspects" a causal relationship. |
| 3 | Two days for widespread infringement or serious disruption of critical infrastructure | supported | Art. 73(3) with Art. 3(49)(b): "serious and irreversible disruption". |

| 5 | Full cooperation, including not altering the system in ways that hamper investigation | supported | Art. 73(6): provider must not alter the system in a way that may affect later evaluation "prior to informing the competent authorities". Deployer cooperation: Art. 26(12). |
| 6 | Serious incident defined by consequence: death or serious harm to health, irreversible infrastructure disruption, fundamental-rights infringement, serious damage to property or environment | supported | Art. 3(49). ### Notes for the lead
- The quotation is cut from mid-sentence; the brief's lead-in ("where deployers have reason to think a system presents a risk") paraphrases the opening of the same sentence, which reads "may result in that AI system presenting a risk within the meaning of Article 79(1)".
- "irreversible disruption of critical infrastructure" in the figure paragraph shortens "serious and irreversible disruption of the management or operation of critical infrastructure".
- The brief writes "at once" for the statute's "forthwith" (reg. 9).
- Body 283 words; one quotation; ten reading-list items.
