against the primary source text. No NotebookLM notebook yet; sources were read directly.

| # | Claim | Finding |

### Disagreements with the first pass
The briefing presents data protection fairness and Equality Act discrimination as one continuous duty. The ICO
treats them as separate regimes. The new brief keeps them apart.
