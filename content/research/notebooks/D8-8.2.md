Method: content/research/METHOD.md, including "Lessons from Domain 3" and "Citations for the source directory".
Every claim in the existing briefing (src/lib/articles.ts, same id) was listed and checked against source text read
on 2 October 2026. Web pages were fetched with curl and read as raw text with the tags stripped; PDFs were read as

## Not verified, and cautions

- Entry into force of Regulation (EU) 2026/1560: the text says the third day after publication. 24 September 2026 is
  my arithmetic, not a printed date. The brief gives only the publication date.
- The cost figures are estimates for EU firms, in euros, drawn by the Commission from a cost-benefit analysis of the
  2024 standard. The underlying analysis was not opened. They are not UK costs.
- The criterion's level text lists "energy, emissions, waste, workforce, safety, pay, conduct"; the brief adds water,
  which is B6.
- "No UK equivalent for small firms was found" is a negative finding from the sources read.
- Whether a UK firm may formally "apply" the standard: the 2025 Recommendation said third-country SMEs may use it;
  the 2026 regulation is silent. Nothing prevents voluntary use, but that is my reading, not a quoted rule.
- "Energy used by AI systems is part of the footprint" is reasoning, not a sourced statement. B3 covers the firm's
  own energy and Scope 1 and 2; cloud-hosted AI would fall under Scope 3, which is outside the Basic Module.
- Body word count: 286 including the six bold labels (267 without). Reading list: 9 items.
