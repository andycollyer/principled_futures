The AI Act deadlines have been delayed. Your obligations have not.

The Digital Omnibus pushed the heaviest requirements to 2027 and 2028 — while leaving two obligations in force that most boards still believe are yet to come. A briefing for the leaders of European SMEs.

2 Feb 2025

Prohibited practices and AI literacy

In force

The Article 5 prohibitions (social scoring, manipulation, untargeted facial scraping) and the Article 4 duty to support staff AI literacy. Applies to deployers — including SMEs using off-the-shelf tools.

2 Aug 2025

GPAI rules and the penalty regime

In force

Obligations for providers of general-purpose AI models; the fines regime applies.

2 Aug 2026

Transparency duties — and AI-literacy oversight

Imminent

Informing chatbot users, labelling deepfakes, notification for emotion recognition or biometric categorisation. Supervision of the AI-literacy duty begins the same day.

2 Dec 2026

New prohibitions and marking of existing systems

This year

A ban on “nudifier” tools and AI-generated child sexual abuse material. Generative systems placed on the market before August 2026 must complete their machine-readable marking.

2 Dec 2027

Stand-alone high-risk systems

Delayed

Recruitment, credit scoring, education, essential services: full risk management, data governance, documentation and human oversight.

2 Aug 2028

AI embedded in regulated products

Delayed

Safety components in medical devices, machinery and vehicles.

The obligation most boards still believe is yet to come

The AI-literacy duty has applied since February 2025 to any organisation whose staff use AI — and its oversight begins in August. If your board cannot demonstrate the training it has provided, that gap is already on the record.

Three moves
1

Inventory every AI system

Including bought tools and AI embedded in existing software. Classify each system against the risk tiers.

2

Document AI literacy

An obligation of effort, not result — but demonstrable effort: who was trained, on what, when.

3

Use the delay well

The deferral of the high-risk rules is preparation time, not a reprieve. Organisations that build their maturity now will meet 2027 as a formality.
